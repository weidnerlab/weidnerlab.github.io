What We Study

Our research explores the diverse applications of entomology, spanning forensic investigations, insect ecology, and wildlife studies.

Forensic Entomology

Investigating insect development and larval morphology to help answer various questions to support criminal investigations.

Learn more
Desert Decomposition

Studying how extreme heat in arid environments affects insect biodiversity, arrival, behavior and colonization dynamics on decomposing remains.

Learn more
Wildlife & Conservation

Using insects as bioindicators to assess ecosystem health and monitor wildlife biodiversity.
