Principal Investigator

Dr. Lauren M. Weidner, Ph.D., D-ABFE

Associate Professor of Forensic Entomology

Dr. Weidner directs the Forensic Entomology and Wildlife Lab (FEWL) at Arizona State University. Her research focuses on blow fly ecology, postmortem interval estimation, and insect-based wildlife monitoring. She is a Diplomate of the American Board of Forensic Entomology and a Fellow of the American Academy of Forensic Science.
