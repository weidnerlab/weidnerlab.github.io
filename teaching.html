Teaching

Arizona State University

School of Interdisciplinary Forensics (SIF), New College of Interdisciplinary Arts and Sciences

Current Courses
FOR 414 ★★ Developed course
Fundamentals of Forensic Entomology
Lecture & Lab — Fall 2019; Fall 2021 & 2022 (lecture only)

Introduction to the use of arthropods in forensic investigations, including postmortem interval estimation, specimen collection, and evidence documentation. Lab component includes insect identification, rearing, and case study analysis.

FOR 200★★ Developed course
Forensic Science Seminar
Spring 2024, 2025, 2027

Seminar course which introduces the student to the forensic science program at ASU along with covering current topics and emerging research in forensic science.

FOR 496★★ Developed course
Forensic Science Service Learning
Spring 2022–2025, 2027

Service-learning course in which students apply forensic science knowledge in a way that assists the community. Iterations of the course have included investigating real cold cases in partnership with law enforcement and advocacy organizations, and the production of forensic science educational content for high schoolers.

Past Courses
FOR 410★ Redesigned course
Testimony and Ethics in Forensic Science
Spring 2021–2023; Fall 2021 & 2022

Examination of ethical standards, professional responsibilities, and expert witness testimony in forensic science. Includes mock trial exercises and case study analysis.

FOR 485
Forensic Science Capstone

Senior capstone course integrating forensic science concepts through independent research projects.

FOR 286 / 105 / 106
Forensic Science Laboratory Courses
FOR 286: Fall 2020 — FOR 105: Fall 2020 — FOR 106: Spring 2020
